Questions buyers ask us


Can foreigners and diaspora buyers purchase property in Kenya?

Yes. Non-citizens can own buildings and leasehold land of up to 99 years. Freehold agricultural land has restrictions. An advocate can handle a purchase remotely.

How do I know a title is genuine?

Your advocate runs an official search at the Ministry of Lands and checks approvals before any deposit is paid.

What extra costs should I budget for?

Stamp duty (4% in urban areas, 2% rural), legal fees, valuation and registration fees. The calculator above gives an estimate.

Do you help with mortgages?

Yes. We introduce you to banks and mortgage advisors and help you compare offers.
